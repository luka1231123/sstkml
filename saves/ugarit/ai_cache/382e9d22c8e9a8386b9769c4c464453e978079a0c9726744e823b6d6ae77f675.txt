To Sinaranu the merchant: thus says Ammurapi, king of Ugarit.  
Let your heart be reassured. Your words have reached my house.  
Do not fear; I have not forgotten you.  
I grant you a portion of silver from the royal treasury.  
Take it in peace, and may the gods bless your journey.  
May your trade flourish and your name be remembered.  
Let this grant stand as a sign of my favor.  
If need be, return to my court when the stars align.  
Until then, may the gods guard you and your household.  
This is the word of Ammurapi, king of Ugarit.
