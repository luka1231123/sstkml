My lord, the granary holds six thousand five hundred seventy-three parisu three qa, and the land yields seed in the ground at eighty-four thousand, though hands supplied fall short by forty-five thousand six hundred. The stores of grain are sufficient for the present, but if the unrest grows or the sea closes, we may find ourselves stretched thin. I would see the overseers of the fields pressed to bring more hands to the harvest before the first rain.
