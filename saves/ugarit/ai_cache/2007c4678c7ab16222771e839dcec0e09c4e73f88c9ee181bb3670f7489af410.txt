My lord the king, I come once more before you with humble plea, as I did in days past when your grace was shown to me. My ship, which comes from Crete, is in need of your favor again, as it has been in times before. I have brought goods that are dear to your court and to your people, and I trust that you will see fit to grant me the exemption once more. I have no other means but to rely upon your kindness, which I know is ever ready for those who serve with loyalty. May your reign be long and your blessings upon all who seek your ear. I remain, my lord the king, your servant and faithful merchant.
