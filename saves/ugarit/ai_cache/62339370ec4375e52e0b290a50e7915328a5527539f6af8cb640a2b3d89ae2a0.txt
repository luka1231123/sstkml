the king, my lord, the fields of ilishtamai are in hand, as they have been each season. we are short by fifty-three hundred labour-days, and the sowing was done with one hundred and forty-eight hundred measures of seed. the work goes on, as it must. the hands are here, as they have always been. the ground is broken, the furrows drawn. the harvest will come, as it always does. the king, my lord, i write as i have written before. the fields of ilishtamai are in hand.
