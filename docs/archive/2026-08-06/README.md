# The sheaf, the threshing floor, and fodder

Removed 2026-08-06. The grain year was four moments: sow, tend, reap to
sheaves, thresh to grain and straw. It is three now -- sow, tend, reap to
grain -- and `HARVEST_PER_1000` carries what the floor used to render.

Why: the reaping window was four fortnights and the threshing window two, so
more was cut every year than the floor could ever take. Sheaves piled at the
seat forever -- 16M qa at year 4, 77M by year 20, growing exactly 15.36M a
year. Fodder had the same shape with nothing at all to eat it: 14.7bn qa.

`farm_with_threshing.py` is the last version that had them.
